Materials & the Environment

An open, interactive book on choosing what things are made of when mass, cost and the planet all sit on the axes. From Ashby's charts to learned latent spaces, eco-indicators, 1 516 simulated jet-engine brackets and a small language model taught by the book itself.

The lab

Materials & the Environment gathers teaching and research on sustainable structural design at ISAE-SUPAERO, Toulouse. Students and researchers build open notebooks and datasets; this site turns them into readable, explorable chapters.

Material selection

Ashby charts, performance indices and multi-objective value functions, extended with eco-costs and eco-indicators.

Machine learning for design

Variational autoencoders that turn material databases into continuous, searchable spaces, with honest tests of what they cannot predict.

Ecodesign & topology optimisation

Shape and material chosen together, with lifetime carbon, energy and water attached to every design.
